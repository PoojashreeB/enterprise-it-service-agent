TITLE: VPN Troubleshooting Guide

CATEGORY: VPN

PURPOSE:
This guide provides standard troubleshooting steps for common
corporate VPN connectivity problems.

--------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 1: VPN IS NOT CONNECTING
--------------------------------------------------

Symptoms:
- VPN client does not connect.
- VPN connection fails.
- User cannot access company applications.

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify that the device has an active internet connection.

2. Open a browser and verify that normal websites can be accessed.

3. Close the corporate VPN application completely.

4. Reopen the VPN application.

5. Sign in using the corporate credentials.

6. If Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is requested,
   complete the MFA verification.

7. Try connecting to the VPN again.

8. If the VPN still does not connect, note the exact
   error message displayed by the VPN client.

--------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 2: VPN AUTHENTICATION FAILURE
--------------------------------------------------

Symptoms:
- VPN displays an authentication error.
- Corporate credentials are rejected.
- MFA authentication fails.

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify that the correct corporate username is being used.

2. Verify that the password is correct.

3. Check whether the corporate password has expired.

4. Complete MFA authentication if prompted.

5. Close and reopen the VPN client.

6. Try authentication again.

7. If authentication continues to fail, contact the IT
   Service Desk for further assistance.

--------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 3: VPN KEEPS DISCONNECTING
--------------------------------------------------

Symptoms:
- VPN connects successfully but disconnects frequently.
- Connection drops while using company applications.

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify that the internet connection is stable.

2. Check whether other websites or applications are
   experiencing connectivity problems.

3. Restart the VPN client.

4. Reconnect to the corporate VPN.

5. If the connection continues to drop, record the
   approximate time and frequency of the disconnects.

6. Contact the IT Service Desk if the problem continues.

--------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 4: VPN IS CONNECTED BUT COMPANY APPLICATIONS
ARE NOT ACCESSIBLE
--------------------------------------------------

Symptoms:
- VPN shows as connected.
- Company applications cannot be accessed.

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify that the VPN connection is still active.

2. Disconnect and reconnect to the VPN.

3. Close and reopen the affected company application.

4. Try accessing the application again.

5. If the application remains inaccessible, record the
   application name and any error message displayed.

6. Contact the IT Service Desk for further investigation.

--------------------------------------------------
SECURITY GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------

- Never share your password with the Service Desk Agent.
- Never provide MFA codes or approval codes.
- Do not disable corporate security controls.
- Do not install unapproved VPN software.
- Follow only approved corporate VPN procedures.

--------------------------------------------------
ESCALATION
--------------------------------------------------

Escalate to the IT Service Desk when:

- VPN authentication continues to fail.
- VPN repeatedly disconnects.
- VPN connects but multiple corporate applications
  remain inaccessible.
- The VPN client displays an unknown or unexpected error.
- The documented troubleshooting steps do not resolve
  the problem.
