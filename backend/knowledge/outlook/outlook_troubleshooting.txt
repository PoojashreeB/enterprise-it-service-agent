TITLE: Outlook Troubleshooting Guide

CATEGORY: OUTLOOK

PURPOSE:
Provide standard troubleshooting guidance for common
corporate Outlook issues.

--------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 1: OUTLOOK IS NOT OPENING
--------------------------------------------------

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Close Outlook completely.
2. Wait a few seconds and try opening Outlook again.
3. Restart the computer.
4. Open Outlook again after the restart.
5. If Outlook still does not open, contact the IT Service Desk.

--------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 2: CANNOT SEND OR RECEIVE EMAILS
--------------------------------------------------

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify that the device has an active internet connection.
2. Check whether Outlook shows "Disconnected" or "Working Offline".
3. If Outlook is offline, reconnect to the network.
4. Restart Outlook.
5. Try sending a test email.
6. If the problem continues, contact the IT Service Desk.

--------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 3: OUTLOOK IS NOT SYNCING
--------------------------------------------------

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify the internet connection.
2. Restart Outlook.
3. Check whether Outlook is showing a synchronization error.
4. Allow some time for synchronization to complete.
5. If synchronization continues to fail, contact the IT Service Desk.

--------------------------------------------------
SECURITY GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------

- Never share your password.
- Never share MFA codes.
- Do not install unofficial Outlook plugins.
- Do not disable corporate security controls.
